A quanity Surveyor is the financial manager and the controller of a construction Project

If a massive wedding requires 500 guests;
    The Architect draws what the venue, the stage, tables and chairs should looks like
    The Engineer makes sure the tent does not collapse on heavy wind
    The Quantity Surveyor calculates exactly how many yards of fabric, plates, and chairs are needed
     negotiates prices with the caterers, and pays the vendors for the amount of work actually completed


On a construction site, a QS manages the money from initial budget estimation before breaking ground, all the way to final audit after completion

            His work on the Job
    ======================================
    1. Estimating and Cost Planning- Calc the total cost of a project before it starts based on architectural drawings
    2. Preparing the Bill of Quantities (BOQ)- Creaating an itemized list of every material, piece of equipment, and labor task needed
    3. Tendering & Procurement - Sending BOQs to contractors to get competitive bids and selecting the right suppliers
    4. Valuation &Site Measurement - Visits the site, and measure the work completed (e.g., "50% of the foundation is done"), and issues Payment certificates.
    5. Cost Control &Variation Management - Tracking design changes or unexpected expenses so the project doesn't excceed it's budget
